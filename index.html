05 — About

Hi, I'm Den. Most people call me Ceebs.

I was born in Kota Kinabalu and spent half my childhood in Labuan, and I still split my time between the two. Somewhere along the way I fell for old cameras and the look of film.

I shoot on a Fujifilm because it's the closest thing to film without the cost of a roll. I'm drawn to quiet light: streetlamps, café glow, the last bit of sun over the water. Those small, honest moments are what I try to catch for you.
